THE FIRST ROUTE

Four places worth the trip

A balanced 10-day loop from city energy to island air. Choose two or three bases and leave room for wandering.

01 / CITY

Seoul

Gyeongbokgung, Bukchon, Hongdae, Namsan Tower and the food alleys of Gwangjang Market.

3–4 days ↗
02 / COAST

Busan

Haedong Yonggungsa, Gamcheon Culture Village, Haeundae Beach and fresh seafood.

2–3 days ↗
03 / HISTORY

Gyeongju

Ancient tombs, Bulguksa Temple and a slower look at Korea's Silla-era history.

1–2 days ↗
04 / ISLAND

Jeju

Hallasan trails, black-lava coastlines, waterfalls and a reset after the big cities.

2–3 days ↗

How to shape the trip

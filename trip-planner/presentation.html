A simple trip planner that turns one destination into a practical seven-day starting plan.

The ideaWhat works nowWhat comes next

01 · THE PROBLEM

Trip research is scattered.

01 / TOO MANY TABS

Places

Travelers search across maps, articles, and guides just to decide what to see.

02 / HARD TO ESTIMATE

Spending

Daily costs are easy to overlook until the itinerary is already taking shape.

03 / NOT CONNECTED

Trip details

Weather, local tips, and hotel search often live in separate tools.

The opportunity: give travelers one clear place to start planning, without pretending to replace specialist booking services.

02 · THE PROPOSED SOLUTION

One destination.
A useful first plan.

INPUT

Where are you going?

Search a city, country, or named attraction. A country search starts in its capital.

OUTPUT

What should you know?

Get a seven-day route, nearby places, a spending estimate, forecast guidance, local apps, and a hotel-search link.

The product is a planning companion: it helps organize research and points users to external services for reservations.

04 · WHAT IS ALREADY BUILT

The core experience is taking shape.

DESTINATION

Search and resolution

City suggestions, country and capital lookup, plus named attraction handling.

DISCOVERY

Nearby places

Popular-place suggestions are gathered from Wikipedia and ranked using Wikidata metadata.

PLANNING

Seven-day view

A day-by-day itinerary, extra places to explore, and estimated local daily spending.

TRAVEL SUPPORT

Useful trip details

Live forecast, travel-app links, hotel-search links, and saved plan restoration in the browser.

05 · WHY IT MATTERS

Less searching.
More confident planning.

ADVANTAGE 01

Time-saving

Brings key early-planning details together around one destination search.

ADVANTAGE 02

Easy to start

A straightforward browser experience with no account required for the core flow.

ADVANTAGE 03

Useful context

Pairs place discovery with weather and a budget estimate before booking decisions.

Its advantage is convenience and orientation—not guaranteed prices, reservations, or authoritative travel advice.

06 · THE PLAN FROM HERE

Build, validate, improve.

NOW · WORKING BASE

Core flow

Destination lookup, nearby place suggestions, seven-day plan, weather and travel links.

NEXT · PROPOSED

Test and refine

Test real destinations and small screens; improve empty, error, and limited-data states.

LATER · PROPOSED

More personal plans

Explore itinerary preferences and clearer budget choices based on user feedback.

The latter two stages are proposed next steps, not features already delivered.

07 · IMPORTANT NOTES & NEXT STEP

A helpful starting point,
with honest limits.

WHAT TO REMEMBER

Estimates are guides

Spending estimates are not live quotes. Hotels open an external search; the planner does not book rooms.

WHAT TO DO NEXT

Validate with travelers

Ask a few people to plan a real trip, note where they hesitate, and prioritize improvements from their feedback.

Place coverage and live data depend on third-party services and internet access. Check local conditions and booking terms before travel.
